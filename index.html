Practising since 2008

Pure health, pure life. That is the promise in the name.

Pura Vida is Val Booth's natural health practice at the Vitality Centre in Mangawhai. Italian born and Australian trained, Val has practised naturopathy, massage and Pilates together since 2008, because bodies rarely have just one thing going on.

The approach is simple. Listen first, test where it helps, then use hands-on treatment, movement and nature's own remedies to bring the body back to balance.

  • Naturopathy, massage and Pilates from one qualified practitioner
  • Listed with Naturopaths & Medical Herbalists of New Zealand
  • Testing available for intolerances, minerals, hormones and heavy metals
