About Pura Vida

One person looking after the whole picture

Pura Vida Natural Health is Val Booth's practice in Mangawhai. Val is a massage therapist, Pilates instructor and naturopath, so she can look at how you move, how you feel and what your body needs, and treat it all in one place.

FAQ

Before you book

Anything else, call or text Val on 021 230 0293.

How do I book?

Call or text Val on 021 230 0293, or send an email. Let her know what you'd like to book and anything that's bothering you, and she'll find a time.

Where are you?

Pura Vida is at 137 Kapawiti Road, Mangawhai, where massage happens in a treatment room surrounded by bush. Ask Val where your Pilates session or class is held when you book.

Which one should I start with?

If you're not sure whether you need a massage, Pilates or a naturopathy consult, tell Val what's going on. She'll suggest a starting point, and you can mix them as you go.

Do I need Pilates experience?

No. Val keeps an eye on your form and can adjust the work to suit your body. Mention any injuries or conditions when you book.

What happens in a naturopathy consult?

Val talks through your health, energy, diet and lifestyle, then suggests natural remedies and practical changes. Ask her about the details and costs when you book.

What should I bring?

For Pilates, wear comfortable clothes you can move in. For massage, just yourself. For a naturopathy consult, bring a list of any medicines or supplements you take.
